Services

Everything you need to operate in Mexico, in clear packages

Each package has a defined scope, a fixed fee and a timeline before we start. Government processing times depend on each authority; we tell you upfront what to expect.

01

Company Setup

For companies opening a Mexican entity.

  • Incorporation, bylaws and powers of attorney
  • Tax ID (RFC) and e.firma
  • Support opening your bank account
  • Employer registration (IMSS) and state payroll tax
  • Your tax compliance calendar, in English
One-time · fixed fee
02

Manufacturing & IMMEX

For manufacturers and maquiladoras.

  • IMMEX program application and maintenance
  • Transition from a shelter to your own operation
  • VAT and IEPS certification
  • Coordination with your customs broker and inventory-control provider
  • Transfer pricing support
One-time · fixed fee
03

Hiring Ready

For your first hires in Mexico.

  • Employment contracts and internal work rules
  • Payroll setup, IMSS and Infonavit
  • Clear briefing on Mexican labor costs: profit sharing (PTU), Christmas bonus, vacation premium and severance
  • Specialized-services (REPSE) review when it applies
One-time · fixed fee
04

Legal & Translation

For contracts and paperwork across two languages.

  • Bilingual commercial and corporate contracts
  • Certified translation of legal documents
  • Coordination with notaries and public brokers
  • Powers of attorney and apostilles
Per project · fixed fee
05

Valuation & Due Diligence

For acquisitions, local partners and joint ventures.

  • Business and asset valuation
  • Financial, tax and legal due diligence
  • Deal structuring and contract review
  • IFRS / US GAAP reconciliation
Per project · fixed fee
06

Ongoing Compliance

Once you are operating — every month.

  • Accounting, monthly and annual tax filings
  • Payroll and social security
  • One-page monthly report in English: what was paid, what is coming, what to watch
  • Direct line for questions, answered within one business day
