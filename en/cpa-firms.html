What we handle

The Mexican side of your clients’ work

When a client’s question turns Mexican, you need someone who knows the rules, works to your standards and answers in English.

01

Entry and setup

When a client starts operating in Mexico.

  • Mexican entity, tax ID (RFC) and employer registration (IMSS)
  • IMMEX program and transition out of a shelter
  • Hiring, payroll and specialized-services (REPSE) review
  • Supplier onboarding with Mexican corporate buyers
One-time · fixed fee
02

Compliance and audits

Once the Mexican operation is running.

  • Monthly accounting, tax filings and payroll
  • SAT and IMSS compliance opinions kept positive
  • Transfer pricing support
  • SAT and IMSS audits, PRODECON and tax litigation
Monthly · fixed fee
03

Deals and valuation

When a client buys, sells or restructures.

  • Tax, financial and legal due diligence on Mexican targets
  • Contingencies quantified for the purchase agreement
  • Valuation of Mexican companies and shares
  • Restructurings and mergers of Mexican entities
Per project · fixed fee
